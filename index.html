What we know about this house

Start from a satellite view

Type the address, open the satellite view, take a screenshot, then use Choose photo below. Edges seen from straight above read true. Sloped edges like gable ends measure a bit short, so check those with a tape.

Open in Google Maps Open in Google Earth

In Maps, switch the layer to Satellite. Google Earth has a ruler tool, so you can measure the house width there and enter it with Set scale. A standard double garage door is about 16 ft wide and a single is about 9 ft.

Take a photo of the house, or pick one from your camera roll. A satellite screenshot works too.
